About Cooper

I am 22 years old. I am born and raised in Sunnyvale, California. In high school, I got my first job at The Golf Club at Moffett Field. I worked my way up there and was offered the assistant professional position while also taking classes at West Valley College. I turned it down to be able to transfer to The University of Iowa. After graduating, I plan on moving to San Diego and hopefully getting a solid job as a data analyst. In my free time, I love to golf, watch sports, and go to the beach.
